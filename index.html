Study Strategies

Noah Zielinski · Amanda Cash · CLC 120 302 · 09/09/26 · Module 3 Core Assignment

Reading Strategy

I personally would use Skimming as my main reading strategy. This is because I don’t have a lot of time in my schedule, and this allows me to save on time while still understanding the structure of the information. I actually already use this strategy, and not only in class. For example, when I am rereading a book because I have some extra time, I usually skim.

Note-Taking Shorthand

I do like decipherable shorthand. Firstly, because it just seems cool to me, to have this mini little “code language” only I understand, and secondly, because it saves time and energy. Writing & instead of and, 2 instead of to, 4 instead of for, etc etc. Less hand fatigue, less time spent.

What I Learned About Memory

I learned that working memory is actually very small. I would have thought it could hold a couple dozen items, but I learned it can hold usually between 5 and 9 items. I also learned that it is very short, a few seconds or up to a minute. I thought it would last up to an hour. I think I was overblowing the scale of working memory. I also learned that long-term memory is basically infinite storage capacity. I was always under the impression that humans lose memory because we start running out of “space” to store memories, but it seems that’s not true.

My Study Methods

I like to use practice testing and concept association in my study sessions. I usually study by making a study guide, uploading it to a tool like quizlet, and running through it a bunch. When I get a question wrong, I look through the actual answer and associate it with something around me, or maybe a similar word or phrase or experience.

My Test-Taking Plan

I will approach my test taking in the same way outlined. I will get to the location a few minutes early, and take a few calming breaths while getting my supplies ready. I will answer all the required questions first, prioritizing the questions I know with the least amount of understanding. I will go over the test once or twice at the end, to make sure I didn’t make any accidental mistakes, provided I have the time. And I will study the results of my test, what I got right and wrong.
